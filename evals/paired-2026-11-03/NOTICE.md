# Evidence scope and rights

The briefs, fictional names/data, generated code, SVG artwork, and actual rendered captures are original material distributed under the accompanying MIT license. They describe local demonstrations, not real festivals, equipment loans, books, bicycle orders, or client products.

Research pages linked in the method retain their own rights. No third-party screenshots, paid course content, competitor implementations, or private project source are included.

All final pilot outputs are retained. Agent handoffs are self-reports from their isolated workspaces; shared captures, maintainer task checks, and reviewer records are identified separately. The results do not establish a general quality ranking, accessibility conformance, or production readiness.
