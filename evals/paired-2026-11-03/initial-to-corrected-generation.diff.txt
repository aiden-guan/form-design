+## Begin without the tells
+
+Build the hierarchy with actual content, composition, natural type metrics, imagery, color roles, alignment, and useful space. Start without the following decorative defaults. Add an exception only when a user-selected reference/brand requires it or the actual task needs its meaning. An aesthetic adjective, a familiar genre, or “it adds polish” is insufficient justification.
+
+| Supplied signal | Starting point |
+| --- | --- |
+| Tags & pills | Plain labels and clear controls; shape is not needed to make every category look important. |
+| Excessive italics | Upright typography; no italic word or phrase added to enliven a headline or cover. |
+| Instrument Serif | Deliberately chosen real-content type roles; no automatic “tasteful” serif substitution. |
+| Negative kerning | Natural tracking; no compressed display-wordmark recipe. |
+| Over-detailed UI | The real task and honest evidence; no decorative mock controls or analytics. |
+| Overused dividers | Group through proximity and alignment; add a rule only for a relationship that needs it. |
+| Shades & opacities | A small semantic palette and legible text; no ladder of faded micro-labels. |
+| Bordered containers | Unboxed composition; keep necessary input/selection boundaries. |
+| Over-detailed diagrams | One useful question per diagram; do not replace a visual concept with a fake technical drawing. |
+| Redundant text | State each fact once; each remaining line adds information. |
+| Code breadcrumbs | The audience's task vocabulary; retain real technical identifiers in actual technical tools. |
+| Section numbering | Unnumbered sections unless sequence is meaningful. |
+| Eyebrows | Start with the useful heading; add a separate label only when it conveys distinct necessary context. |
+| ASCII images | Subject-specific evidence or artwork instead of a terminal costume. |
+| Squiggles | No loose ornamental marks, doodles, or hand-drawn emphasis. |
+| Excessive mono | Readable interface/body type; reserve mono for a genuine technical role. |
+| ALL CAPS subtitles | Sentence case supporting text. |
+| Gradient backgrounds | A deliberate flat surface; use a gradient only for a demonstrated material/data/reference need. |
+| Periods in short UI labels | Plain action/navigation labels; preserve supplied sentences and necessary punctuation. |
+
+Two additional defaults were exposed in FORM's own rejected outputs: **indicator lights** and **arrow glyphs**. Do not add colored dots beside labels to suggest activity, availability, or sophistication. Show genuine status in words. Do not append `↗`, `→`, chevrons, or arrow icons to ordinary buttons, links, list rows, or captions. Use clear action labels. A native disclosure control or a genuinely directional task can justify its conventional affordance; an ordinary “Open” action needs no arrow.
+
+These two additions come from direct owner feedback, not the Charles Liu attribution. The original 19-signal selection remains separate from the reference catalog, and dithering remains excluded.
