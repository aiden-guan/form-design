# Attribution and scope

FORM Design is a portable skill created from the public-reference portion of the FORM Design Library. Its original instructions, summaries, examples, and tools are offered under [LICENSE.txt](LICENSE.txt).

The reference index is a discovery aid. Creator names, work titles, attributed descriptions, and source URLs identify third-party work; inclusion grants no rights to that work. Each record preserves its source and recorded rights label. Inspect the original license before copying anything. No creator media, full essays, paid course material, fonts, private project code, or source bundles are included.

Research sources and bounded inspection notes are in [designer-principles.md](references/designer-principles.md). The summaries and applications are FORM's interpretation; the named practitioners do not endorse this skill.

`data/manifest.json` records the snapshot, collection counts, public handoff base, and build-input hashes. The catalog consists of public-reference metadata only. Imported metadata is not evidence that every visual or interaction was independently inspected. The live original and its current terms remain authoritative.

The reference sources include ascii.rest, Best Designs on X, 60fps.design, Bencho, Svelte Animations, mkdir.directory, React Bits, Motion Examples, Design Spells, and individually attributed creator references. The actual collections and counts are listed by `scripts/search.mjs --stats` rather than promised as a permanently current catalog size.
